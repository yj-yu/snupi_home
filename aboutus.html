SNU PI Lab investigates multimodal agentic AI and world foundation models. Our central question is how agents can perceive complex environments, reason over what they observe, and act reliably across digital and physical systems.

We Mainly Work On

Multimodal Agents

Autonomous agents that perceive across language, vision, video, and audio, grounding reasoning, planning, and tool use in real digital environments.

World Foundation Models

Generative models that learn how the world evolves under actions, providing the basis for prediction, planning, and embodied decision making.

Physical Intelligence

World action models and vision-language-action learning that couple prediction with control, enabling agents to act reliably on system.

Efficient On-Device Intelligence

Quantization, distillation, and efficient multimodal architectures that bring agents to edge devices and robots, enabling real-time intelligence beyond the cloud.

Safe & Human-Centered AI

Safety evaluation and alignment of foundation models, toward AI that stays reliable and beneficial across diverse users and languages.

Advancing research through government-funded projects and industry collaborations.
