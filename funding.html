SNU PI Lab develops intelligent systems that perceive, reason, and act across digital and physical environments. Our core strength is multimodal agentic AI. We build on this to advance world models and extend toward physical intelligence.

Our Research Areas

Multimodal Agentic AI

Foundation models and agents that integrate language, vision, video, and more to perceive, reason, plan, and solve complex tasks.

World Models

Models that learn environment dynamics and predict the outcome of actions, enabling planning, interaction, and embodied intelligence.

Physical Intelligence

Vision-language-action models and robot learning that translate perception and reasoning into real-world action.

Reliable & Human-Centered AI

Safe, aligned, and human-centered AI systems grounded in AI safety research, ensuring reliability in real-world interaction.

Our Mission

Building intelligence that connects perception, reasoning, and action to create real impact.
